5:48 am · offshore wind · A-frame peak

Dawn Patrol

The set is standing up on the peak. Stay ahead of the whitewater, pump for speed, launch off the lip and tuck into the barrel.

  • TakeoffYou drop in at the peak. Press ← or → to go left or right; the wave peels the way you pick.
  • ↑ ↓ rail turnsHold ↑ to carve toward the top, ↓ toward the bottom. Keep holding and the turn keeps going round, any direction you like. ← → point you along the wave either way.
  • Space pumpTap in rhythm to pump down the line. Works best high on the face.
  • Hit the lipBottom turn, then carve up into the lip to launch: riding straight up just goes over the top. The faster you go and the bigger the wave, the bigger the air.
  • AirsThe angle and speed you hit the lip decide the air. Keep holding ↑ or ↓ to keep rotating (alley-oop), or spin with ← →. Land nose first or tail first; tap ← or → to revert from fakie.
  • Air tricksShift grabs: alone = Indy, with ↑ = Slob, ↓ = Tail grab, ←/→ = Stalefish. Rodeo flip: grab with Shift, then hold ↑/↓ and ←/→ together. Space in the air for a Christ air: arms out in a cross; it has to finish before you land or you land stiff.
  • Wave tricksTurn hard off the top at speed and the fins let go a little: the tail skids and sprays (chip the lip). Carve hard low on the face for a bottom turn that fires you up faster. Bury a full-rail carve through the middle of the face = gouge. Ride the top of the breaking part = floater; ride the top of a throwing lip = lip grind. Turns back into the curl are all yours to time. Shift in a hard turn = layback.
  • RocksCarve round them or air over them. When a barrel section starts the reef drains, then the surge buries it in a boil: hit the boil fast and it launches you.
  • Barrel sectionWhen the wave starts pitching, hold Shift to stall so the lip throws over you. Ride low inside.
  • C or 1–4Switch view: beach, chase, first person, drone.
